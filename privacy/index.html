Privacy policy

Last updated October 21, 2026

This policy explains what personal information Flowsent collects, where it comes from, what we do with it, and the choices you have. Flowsent is run by Irakli Morchadze, who is responsible for your information. For anything about your data, email hello@flowsent.ai.

If you visit this website

  • This site has no forms, and we don't use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, such as your IP address, browser and the pages you request. They're used to keep the site secure and running.
  • The booking calendar and our videos are embedded from a scheduling service and a video hosting service. They can set their own cookies and record things like whether a video was played and for how long, under their own privacy policies. We use the viewing statistics to improve our videos.

If you book a call

We collect what you enter when you book: your name, email address, phone number and the time you choose. We use it to hold the call, to send you the invite and reminders by email and text message, and to prepare for our conversation, which can include looking at public information about your company. We keep it while we're talking about working together and delete it once it's no longer needed, unless you become a client.

If we've sent you an email

Flowsent sends personal, one-to-one business emails to people whose role and company suggest our service could be useful to them. When we run campaigns for a client, we do this on the client's behalf and under their instructions.

  • What we collect. Your name, job title, company, work email address and business details, plus public information about you and your company, such as posts, interviews, podcasts and company news.
  • Where it comes from. Professional networking sites, company websites, other public sources, and business contact data providers. We check email addresses with a verification service before we send anything.
  • Why we use it. To send you a short series of business emails and to handle your replies. We rely on our legitimate interest in reaching businesses that may benefit from our services, and we keep the emails relevant to your work.
  • Automation. We use software to research companies, check whether they fit, and draft emails. Nothing in this process makes decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you.
  • How to stop it. Reply "not interested" or "unsubscribe" to any of our emails, or write to hello@flowsent.ai. We'll stop emailing you and add your email address, or your company's domain, to a do-not-contact list so it doesn't happen again.
  • How long we keep it. As long as a campaign needs it, and no longer than 12 months after our last email to you. The do-not-contact list is kept for as long as we operate, because that's how we make sure we don't email you again.

If you're a client

We use the information you share with us, such as your offer, your ideal customer, any customer lists, and your contract and billing details, to deliver the service and to bill you. We keep it for as long as we work together, and afterwards only as long as the law requires, for example for tax and accounting records.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it for advertising.

We use service providers to run Flowsent: website hosting, scheduling, email sending and inboxes, data storage and workflow automation, contact data and email verification, video hosting, and research and writing software. They process your information only to provide their service to us.

Some of these providers are in the United States and other countries, so your information may be processed outside the country where you live. Where the law requires it, we rely on the safeguards these providers offer, such as standard contractual clauses. We may also disclose information when the law requires us to.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to:

  • show you the personal information we hold about you
  • correct it
  • delete it
  • stop using it, or limit how we use it
  • send you a copy in a format you can take elsewhere

You can object to direct marketing at any time, and we'll stop. To make any of these requests, email hello@flowsent.ai. We'll answer within 30 days. If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the data protection authority where you live.

Security

We protect your information with reasonable measures. Access is limited to the people and services that need it, and data is sent over encrypted connections.

Children

Flowsent is a business service and isn't meant for anyone under 16. We don't knowingly collect information about children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions or requests: hello@flowsent.ai. Our terms of use cover the rest of how this site works.
