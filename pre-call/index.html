    The six questions people ask most before a call like this. Open the ones that matter to you.

    What makes you different from other lead gen agencies?

    You get booked calls, not a list of leads. A lot of agencies hand you a spreadsheet of "interested" contacts and leave the rest to you. We handle every reply ourselves, answer the questions, and a call only counts once it's on your calendar.

    We also do the research before we write: every prospect is checked against your ideal customer first, and every email opens with something specific to that one person. And you work with me. I'm on your strategy call and I run your campaigns after it, with no hand-off to an account manager you've never met. If you don't have 15 booked calls by day 30 of launch, I keep working at no extra cost until you do.

    What results can we expect, and how fast?

    Fifteen booked calls with qualified buyers in your first 30 days live. The first two weeks are setup: we agree on your ideal customer and your messaging, and we warm up the inboxes we send from so the emails land. Nothing goes out to prospects during that time. Then we launch, and that's day one of your 30 days.

    A booked call means a decision maker at a company that matches the ideal customer we agreed on, booked on your calendar. Not a lead, and not someone who said "maybe". If you're short of 15 on day 30, I keep working at no extra cost until you have all 15.

    Will this hurt our domain or land us in spam?

    No. Your main domain never sends a single cold email. Before launch we set up separate domains, close variations of your company name, each with its own inboxes, and warm them up for two weeks so inbox providers learn to trust them before any cold email goes out.

    Once we're live, each inbox sends only a small number of emails a day and we watch how they land. If one starts to slip, we pause it and the others carry on. Even if a sending domain ever had a problem, it couldn't touch the address your clients and your team use. The emails help too: templates sent to thousands of people get flagged, while researched, personal emails get replies.

    How much of our team's time does this take?

    Very little, and almost all of it in the first two weeks. You fill in an intake form, about 30 minutes, so I understand your product, your best customers and how you sell. Then we do one strategy session to pin down your ideal customer and your messaging, and you approve the messaging before anything goes out.

    Everything else is on our side: the domains, the inboxes, the research, the emails and every reply. After launch, your part is to show up to the calls and close them. You're notified every time a call books, and once a week we go through the numbers together.

    We've been burned by an agency before. Why is this different?

    When an agency goes wrong, it's usually one of three things: the sending was set up badly so everything went to spam, they sent one template to a bought list, or they handed you a spreadsheet of "leads" and called that a result. We built around all three.

    We send from separate domains that we warm up for two weeks. Every prospect is checked against your ideal customer, and every email is written around that person. You approve the messaging before anything goes out, and a call only counts once it's booked on your calendar. If we fall short of 15 by day 30 of launch, I keep working at no extra cost until you have them. On the call, tell me what went wrong last time: it will show us quickly whether this is different for you.

    Our buyers already get dozens of cold emails. Does this still work?

    Yes, and the reason they get so many is the reason ours stand out. Most of those emails are one template sent to thousands of people, and a busy founder spots one in about two seconds.

    Ours start somewhere else. First we check that the person actually fits your ideal customer. Then we read up on them: a podcast they were on, something they posted, something their company just shipped. The email opens with that, so it reads like it was written for them, because it was. When everything else in the inbox looks the same, the one email that's clearly about them is the one they answer. If you booked this call from one of our emails, you've already seen it work once.
