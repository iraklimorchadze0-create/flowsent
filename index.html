Questions people ask before booking

Anything else, ask me on the call or at hello@flowsent.ai.

What does Flowsent do?

Flowsent books sales calls for B2B SaaS companies with personal cold email, done for you. We find the companies that match your ideal customer, research each person, write to them one by one, answer the replies, and put the interested ones on your calendar. The promise is 15 booked sales calls in your first 30 days live.

Who is Flowsent for?

B2B companies where one new client is worth thousands and deals start with a conversation, starting with B2B SaaS. It's not a fit if you sell to consumers or haven't sold your offer yet, because booked calls aren't your bottleneck then. If that's you, we'll say so on the first call.

How does the process work?

There are two stages. For the first two weeks we set up separate sending domains and inboxes, warm them up, agree on your ideal customer with you and build the list. Then we launch, and three systems do the work. The ICP Engine researches and scores every prospect. The Personalization Engine writes each one a personal email. The Custom Response System answers interested replies within minutes and books the call.

How fast will we see results?

Calls start once we launch, two weeks after you sign. Those two weeks are for setup and inbox warm-up, because skipping them is how emails end up in spam. Your 30-day clock starts on launch day, and the guarantee is 15 booked calls within those 30 days.

What happens if you don't book 15 calls in 30 days?

We keep working at no extra cost until you have all 15. The guarantee is written into your contract, not just stated on this page.

What counts as a booked call?

A decision maker at a company that matches the ideal customer we agree on together, booked on your calendar. The 30 days start on launch day, after the two-week setup.

Will cold email hurt my domain reputation?

No. We send from separate domains that we buy, warm up, and monitor. Your main domain never sends a cold email, so its reputation is never at risk.

Is this better than hiring an SDR?

It's faster to start and carries less risk. A full-time SDR costs close to $100k a year with benefits and tools, takes three to six months to ramp, and if they leave, you start over. With Flowsent you pay one monthly fee, with no salaries and no ad spend, and calls start after a two-week build.

How is Flowsent different from other lead generation agencies?

Most agencies buy a list, send one template to everyone, and hand you a spreadsheet of leads. We research every prospect, write to each one personally, answer replies within minutes, and book the calls for you. And we guarantee the number.

What do you need from me?

About 30 minutes for the intake form, one strategy session, and a quick approval of the messaging before launch. After that, your job is to show up to the calls.

How much does Flowsent cost?

It depends on your market and how many people we need to reach. We work that out on the first call, then walk you through a plan with exact pricing on a short follow-up call.
